# Slide 1: Introduction

## TeleBot AI: The Future of Multilingual Support
*   **Project Overview**: An intelligent, 100% offline multilingual telecom assistant designed for privacy and high performance.
*   **Core Concept**: Bringing state-of-the-art LLMs and Text-to-Speech (TTS) models directly to local hardware.
*   **Key Value Proposition**: Real-time voice interaction in regional Indian languages without needing an internet connection or cloud APIs.
