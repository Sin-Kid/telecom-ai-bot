# Slide 2: Problem Statement

## The Challenges of Modern AI Bots
1.  **Internet Dependency**: Most AI assistants fail in remote areas or during network outages.
2.  **Privacy Concerns**: Sensitive user data is often sent to external cloud servers (OpenAI, Sarvam, etc.).
3.  **High Latency & Costs**: Real-time voice synthesis in Indian languages is expensive and often suffers from cloud-induced delays.
4.  **Language Barriers**: Limited support for high-quality, natural-sounding voice output in regional dialects like Kannada, Tamil, or Telugu.
